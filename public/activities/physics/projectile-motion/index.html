Which of the following statements is/are correct?

Two small balls A and B are launched simultaneously from the top of a building. Air resistance is negligible and both motions are in the same plane.

A Horizontal launch

Initial velocity: 10 m s⁻¹ horizontally

B Angled launch

Initial velocity: 20 m s⁻¹ at 60° above horizontal

  1. 1Balls A and B travel equal vertical distances in equal times.
  2. 2Balls A and B travel equal horizontal distances in equal times.
  3. 3Balls A and B never meet.
Answer options

Why D is correct

  1. Resolve B's velocity. Its horizontal component is 20 cos 60° = 10 m s⁻¹, exactly the same as A's horizontal velocity.
  2. Compare vertical motion. A starts with vertical velocity 0, while B starts with 20 sin 60° = 10√3 m s⁻¹ upward. Their vertical distances are therefore not equal.
  3. Check whether they meet. They start at the same point, but after launch B is always higher than A for t > 0. They do not meet again while travelling in air.
