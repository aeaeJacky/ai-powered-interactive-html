Frustum & Cone
Original Question Paper

Problem Statement

A vessel in the form of a frustum is made by cutting off the lower part of an inverted right circular cone of base radius \(72\text{ cm}\) and height \(96\text{ cm}\). The height of the vessel is \(60\text{ cm}\). The vessel is placed on a horizontal table. Some water is poured into the vessel. The depth of water in the vessel is \(28\text{ cm}\).

GIVEN INFORMATION

Original Cone — Base Radius
\(R = 72\text{ cm}\)
Original Cone — Height
\(H = 96\text{ cm}\)
Vessel (Frustum) — Height
\(h = 60\text{ cm}\)
Water Depth
\(d = 28\text{ cm}\)

Questions

(a) Find the area of the wet curved surface of the vessel in terms of \(\pi\).
(b) John claims that the volume of water in the vessel is greater than \(0.1\text{ m}^3\). Do you agree? Explain your answer.

Part (a) Progress

Part (a): Wet Curved Surface Area

Cross-section of the frustum vessel with water — the shaded cone below is the part cut off (Part (a) Step 1).

1
Identify the small cone that was cut off
▼
The vessel height is \(60\text{ cm}\), so the small cone (cut off from the bottom of the inverted cone) has height: $$h_{\text{small}} = 96 - 60 = 36\text{ cm}$$
Think about the full cone vs. what remains after cutting. The frustum is the "top" 60 cm portion; the cut-off piece is the bottom 36 cm.
By similar triangles (cross-section of the cone): $$\frac{r_{\text{small}}}{36} = \frac{72}{96}$$ $$r_{\text{small}} = \frac{72 \times 36}{96} = 27\text{ cm}$$
2
Find the radius at the water level
▼
The frustum's side is a straight line. The radius varies linearly from 27 cm (bottom) to 72 cm (top) over 60 cm.
The radius of the vessel at height \(h\) from the bottom: $$r(h) = 27 + \frac{72 - 27}{60}\,h = 27 + \frac{3}{4}\,h$$
At the water level (\(h = 28\text{ cm}\)): $$r(28) = 27 + \frac{3}{4} \times 28 = 27 + 21 = 48\text{ cm}$$
3
Calculate the slant height of the wet portion
▼
Use Pythagoras' theorem. The horizontal difference in radius is \(48 - 27 = 21\) cm and the vertical height is \(28\) cm.
$$s = \sqrt{(48 - 27)^2 + 28^2} = \sqrt{21^2 + 28^2} = \sqrt{441 + 784} = \sqrt{1225} = 35\text{ cm}$$
4
Compute the wet curved surface area
▼
The curved surface area of a frustum: \(\text{CSA} = \pi(r_1 + r_2) \times s\)

Here \(r_1 = 27\text{ cm}\), \(r_2 = 48\text{ cm}\), \(s = 35\text{ cm}\): $$\text{CSA} = \pi(27 + 48) \times 35 = \pi \times 75 \times 35$$

Answer (a)

\(\text{Wet curved surface area} = 2625\pi\text{ cm}^2\)

Part (b) Progress

Part (b): Volume of Water — Agree or Disagree?

The water itself forms a frustum — bottom radius 27 cm, top radius 48 cm at the water level, height 28 cm.

1
Identify the wet volume as a frustum
▼
The water fills a smaller frustum inside the vessel with:

\(\bullet\) Bottom radius \(r = 27\text{ cm}\) (same as vessel bottom)
\(\bullet\) Top radius \(R = 48\text{ cm}\) (radius at water level)
\(\bullet\) Height \(h = 28\text{ cm}\)
2
Apply the frustum volume formula
▼
Frustum volume \(= \dfrac{\pi h}{3}(R^2 + Rr + r^2)\). This is equivalent to the volume of the large cone minus the small cone.
$$V = \frac{\pi h}{3}\left(R^2 + Rr + r^2\right)$$ $$= \frac{\pi \times 28}{3}\left(48^2 + 48 \times 27 + 27^2\right)$$ $$= \frac{28\pi}{3}\left(2304 + 1296 + 729\right)$$ $$= \frac{28\pi}{3} \times 4329$$
3
Calculate and convert to m³
▼
$$V = \frac{28 \times 4329}{3}\,\pi = \frac{121212}{3}\,\pi = 40404\pi\text{ cm}^3$$
Convert to \(\text{m}^3\) (\(1\text{ m}^3 = 10^6\text{ cm}^3\)): $$V = \frac{40404\pi}{10^6}\text{ m}^3 = 0.040404\pi\text{ m}^3$$ $$\approx 0.040404 \times 3.14159 \approx 0.1269\text{ m}^3$$

Answer (b)

Since \(V \approx 0.127\text{ m}^3 > 0.1\text{ m}^3\),
We agree with John.
