About Education Demo

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower every student to become a confident, responsible, and engaged citizen through a supportive and inclusive education.

Our History

Founded in 1985, Education Demo has grown from a small neighborhood school into a thriving institution serving more than 1,200 students. Over the years, we have remained committed to academic excellence and community partnership.

Our Values

  • Integrity in all that we do
  • Respect for every individual
  • Excellence in teaching and learning
  • Innovation in our approach
